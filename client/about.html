Our Story

Founded in 2014, Kanta has been providing best snacks products for over a decade. Our journey began with a simple mission: to bring joy to families through delicious, high-quality snacks made with the finest ingredients.

From our humble beginnings in a small factory to becoming a household name across Sri Lanka, we've maintained our commitment to quality, tradition, and innovation. Every product we produce carries the legacy of craftsmanship and care that has defined our brand for years.

Today, we operate a state-of-the-art production facility that combines traditional methods with modern technology, ensuring each product meets our exacting standards. Our commitment to excellence has earned us the trust of customers across the country.

Our Vision

To be the most loved and trusted snacks brand in Sri Lanka, bringing happiness to every home with our exceptional products. We envision a future where Kanta products are synonymous with quality, taste, and family moments.

We aim to expand our reach while maintaining the values that have made us successful: integrity, quality, and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

We are committed to producing the finest quality snacks using premium ingredients and modern technology. We strive to exceed customer expectations while maintaining sustainable and ethical business practices.

Our mission includes:

  • Using only the highest quality ingredients
  • Maintaining strict quality control standards
  • Supporting local farmers and suppliers
  • Minimizing our environmental impact
  • Providing excellent value to our customers
  • Creating a positive workplace for our employees
